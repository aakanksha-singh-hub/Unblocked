So what is the claim, exactly?

Narrower than "cause-matching works".

Most of the advantage is the intake-repair mechanism — finding invoices that never reached the buyer's system. If chasing paperwork rarely unblocks an invoice in reality, most of this evaporates. That is the assumption to attack, and naming it is more useful than a limitations paragraph that lists every parameter equally.
