What each policy recovered on the held-out book, and what it cost to get there.

Every policy faces identical random draws, so the differences are paired rather than two lucky runs.

What this establishes. How well the approach performs given the assumptions the simulation is built on — not evidence those assumptions hold. We wrote the simulator, so beating baselines inside it cannot prove the thesis that shaped it. Commit ordering proves sequence, not independence, and this project does not argue otherwise.
