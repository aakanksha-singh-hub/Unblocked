Where it will not guess

Four rules are plain code, not a model. A language model can be argued with; an if statement cannot.

They promised a date

Silent until then, plus a few days for the transfer to land.

They raised a complaint

Settle it first. Asking again reads as not listening.

They said they can't pay

No pressure. Offer a payment plan instead.

The account is too big to risk

Anything irreversible needs a person to sign it off.
