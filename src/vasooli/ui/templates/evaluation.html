What this establishes. Policy quality conditional on the assumptions in sim/calibration.py — not evidence those assumptions hold. We wrote the simulator, so beating baselines inside it cannot prove the thesis that shaped it. Commit ordering proves sequence, not independence, and this project does not argue otherwise.
