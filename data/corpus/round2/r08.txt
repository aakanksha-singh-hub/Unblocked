Below are 8 situations. For each, just type what you'd ACTUALLY reply if you were the one who owes the money. Short is fine. Hinglish, typos, WhatsApp style - please don't make it formal, that's the whole point.

Please make everything up - no real company names, amounts or transaction numbers. Your replies go into an open dataset used to test whether AI can read messages like these. Skip any you don't want to answer.

1. You are not sure whether this bill has been paid or not and you need to check with your accounts person. The supplier is asking. You have only recently started buying from them.

2. Your company pays all suppliers in one batch on the 10th of each month. This bill missed the last batch. The supplier has messaged asking where the payment is.

3. You have been told by your manager not to release this payment but you have not been told why. The supplier is asking. You have only recently started buying from them.

4. You are waiting for your GST filing to be done before releasing payments this month. The supplier has followed up for the second time. You have only recently started buying from them.

5. The rate on the bill is higher than what was agreed in the purchase order. The supplier is chasing payment.

6. You will have money after a large customer of yours pays you, which you expect sometime near the end of the month. The supplier is asking for an update. You have a long-standing relationship with this supplier.

7. Your own customers owe you a lot of money and have not paid, so you cannot pay your suppliers either. The supplier is following up. This is the first time they have contacted you about it.

8. Your company requires all supplier bills to be uploaded to a vendor portal. This one is not showing up there. The supplier says they sent it by email.
