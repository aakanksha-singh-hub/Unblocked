Hi! Doing a student project on how businesses reply to payment reminders. Can you help - 5 mins?

Below are 8 situations. For each, just type what you'd ACTUALLY reply if you were the one who owes the money. Short is fine. Hinglish, typos, WhatsApp style - please don't make it formal, that's the whole point.

Please make everything up - no real company names, amounts or transaction numbers. Your replies go into an open dataset used to test whether AI can read messages like these. Skip any you don't want to answer.

1. Your company pays all suppliers in one batch on the 10th of each month. This bill missed the last batch. The supplier has messaged asking where the payment is. You have a long-standing relationship with this supplier.

2. The GST number printed on the bill is wrong, so your accounts team cannot process it. The supplier is asking why payment is delayed. The supplier has messaged you once before about this.

3. Business has been very slow for several months and you honestly do not have the cash right now. The supplier is asking for payment. The supplier has messaged you once before about this.

4. Your own customers owe you a lot of money and have not paid, so you cannot pay your suppliers either. The supplier is following up. The supplier has messaged you once before about this.

5. Two of the cartons in the last delivery arrived crushed and you had to reject them. Nobody has sent you a corrected bill. The supplier is asking for full payment. You have a long-standing relationship with this supplier.

6. You have been told by your manager not to release this payment but you have not been told why. The supplier is asking. The supplier has messaged you once before about this.

7. Someone else in your office handles this supplier and you are just replying because they messaged your number. The bill is more than three months overdue.

8. Diwali is coming and your office will be shut for a week. You plan to clear pending bills after reopening. The supplier is asking. This is the first time they have contacted you about it.
