Hi! Doing a student project on how businesses reply to payment reminders. Can you help - 5 mins?

Below are 8 situations. For each, just type what you'd ACTUALLY reply if you were the one who owes the money. Short is fine. Hinglish, typos, WhatsApp style - please don't make it formal, that's the whole point.

Please make everything up - no real company names, amounts or transaction numbers. Your replies go into an open dataset used to test whether AI can read messages like these. Skip any you don't want to answer.

1. The GST number printed on the bill is wrong, so your accounts team cannot process it. The supplier is asking why payment is delayed. You have only recently started buying from them.

2. Your bank shows the transfer went out but the supplier says it has not arrived. They are chasing you. The bill is about three weeks overdue.

3. You will have money after a large customer of yours pays you, which you expect sometime near the end of the month. The supplier is asking for an update.

4. Your company requires all supplier bills to be uploaded to a vendor portal. This one is not showing up there. The supplier says they sent it by email. The bill is more than three months overdue.

5. You paid this bill a few days ago by bank transfer. The supplier seems not to have noticed and is asking again. The bill is more than three months overdue.

6. You have no particular reason for not paying and no clear plan for when you will. The supplier has messaged asking for an update. You have only recently started buying from them.

7. Your own customers owe you a lot of money and have not paid, so you cannot pay your suppliers either. The supplier is following up. The bill is more than three months overdue.

8. You need the e-way bill and delivery challan copies to process this. You don't have them. The supplier is asking for payment. You have only recently started buying from them.
